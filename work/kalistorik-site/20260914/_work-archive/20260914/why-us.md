# Why Us (EN source copy)

## Headline

**Anyone can find you a factory.**

## Subtext

Finding one that stays honest — through the second order, the busy season, the year you are not watching — that is the actual work. Everything we do is built around it.

## Body

Factories do not fail loudly. They fail politely: a queue that quietly reshuffles, a finish that drifts one shade, a "close enough" nobody tells you about. By the time it surfaces at unboxing, the fixing window closed months ago.

Our answer is presence, not promises. We walk production floors in person. We turn down more factories than we take on, and removals from our network are permanent. When your order is running, someone holding your spec — and your authority — stands where the decisions happen, and answers the same day instead of the same week.

The hard part was never finding a factory. It is finding one that stays honest. We have spent two decades doing exactly that, one vetted floor at a time.
