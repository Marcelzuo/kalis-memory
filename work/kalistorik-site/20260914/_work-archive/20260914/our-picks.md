**Title:** Your orders run all year. Your factory visit lasts one week.

**Body:** Between visits, decisions about your goods get made every single day — who goes first in the queue, which batch gets cut, what counts as "close enough." Someone is in the room for those calls. It should be someone on your side. It is us.

## Hook 2 — Independent QC (third-party inspection)

**Title:** A factory will never send you its own bad photos.

**Body:** That is why our inspections come from independent third-party inspectors. They answer to you, not to the workshop. You receive the same report we do — raw photos, video, findings — before the balance is due. Nothing passes on the factory's word alone.

## Hook 3 — Q4 timing

**Title:** A booking confirmation is not a shipping plan.

**Body:** Blank sailings. Port backlogs. The holiday shutdown. The Q4 window does not slam shut — it closes quietly, one small slip at a time, while everyone trusts the dates on paper. We work backward from the gate-in deadline, not forward from a promise.
