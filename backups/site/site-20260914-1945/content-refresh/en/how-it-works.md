# How It Works — 4 Steps (EN source copy)

> Skeleton unchanged: requirements → factory match & freight lock → independent QC → delivery to port.

## Step 1

**Title:** Send Us Your Spec

**Description:** Tell us what you need — style, materials, quantity, budget, deadline. A photo, a drawing, or a reference link works. The clearer the spec, the faster the match. You get a straight answer in plain language, not a sales pitch.

## Step 2

**Title:** Match the Factory, Lock the Freight

**Description:** We shortlist the workshops that actually make your product well — not whoever pays us the most. Before you commit, the ocean freight rate is locked, so your landed cost stops drifting after the deposit.

## Step 3

**Title:** Independent Inspection, Before You Pay

**Description:** Third-party inspectors check your order mid-production, then again before shipment. Photos, video, and a full report go straight to you — unfiltered. The balance is only released after you approve.

## Step 4

**Title:** From Factory Door to Your Port

**Description:** We book the container, watch the loading, seal it, and track it to a major European port. Export documents arrive ready for your customs broker. You always know where your goods are.

## Closing line (optional)

Ready when you are. Send the spec — the rest follows, step by step.
