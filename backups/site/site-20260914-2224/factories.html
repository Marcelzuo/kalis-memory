Why Us

Factory Network

Anyone can find you a factory. Finding one that stays honest — through the second order, the busy season, the year you are not watching — that is the actual work.

01/03

Vetted in person, not by brochure

Every workshop in the network we have walked ourselves. We have seen the lines, met the people who run them, and watched how they behave when an order is late. If we have not stood on the floor, it does not carry your goods.

02/03

A small list, kept that way

We turn down more workshops than we take on, and removals are permanent. The list stays short on purpose: a standard you cannot police across a hundred floors is not a standard, it is a wish.

03/03

Someone on your side while the order runs

When your production is live, decisions get made every day — who goes first in the queue, which batch slips, what counts as close enough. Someone holding your spec, and your authority, is in the room for those calls. They answer the same day, not the same week.

The hard part was never finding a factory. It is finding one that stays honest when nobody is watching. Presence is how we keep that promise — order after order.
