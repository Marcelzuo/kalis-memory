Freight & Logistics

A booking confirmation is not a shipping plan. The plan is every date behind it — worked out backward, and watched on the ground.

01/03

Book the space, lock the rate

We secure space on the sailing and lock the freight rate before you commit, so your landed cost stops drifting after the deposit. Rates move; your number does not move with them.

02/03

Watch the loading and seal

Someone from our side watches the container being loaded: the count, the condition, the seal number noted and photographed before the doors close. What leaves the factory is documented — so nothing is negotiable at destination.

03/03

Work backward from the gate-in deadline

The Q4 window does not slam shut — it closes quietly, one small slip at a time. We plan from the gate-in deadline backward: production, inland transit, port backlog, blank sailings. Every date is derived from the last one, not from a promise. When a step starts to slide, you hear it early enough to still do something about it.

Blank sailings, backlogs, the holiday shutdown — the window closes quietly. Planning backward from the gate-in deadline is how your goods stay inside it.
