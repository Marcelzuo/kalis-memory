# KALIS TORIK — Services

KALIS TORIK provides six linked services that cover a furniture order from specification to European port. Details on the website: https://kalistorik.com/quality and https://kalistorik.com/freight.

## 1. Factory screening

Every workshop in the network has been visited in person: production lines walked, operators met, behavior under deadline pressure observed. If the team has not stood on the floor, the factory does not carry buyers' goods. More factories are turned down than taken on, and removals from the network are permanent. See https://kalistorik.com/factories.

## 2. On-site follow-up

While production runs, someone holding the buyer's spec and authority is present where daily decisions get made — who goes first in the queue, which batch slips, what counts as acceptable. Questions get answered the same day, not the same week.

## 3. Pre-production sample

Photos and a short video of the first piece are shared before mass production starts. The buyer signs off on what the line will actually make, not on a showroom sample.

## 4. Mid-production quality control

Independent third-party inspectors walk the line while the order is still being made — materials, construction, and finish compared against the spec, on the spot. A defect caught here is a rework. The same defect caught at unboxing is a claim, a delay, and a difficult conversation with the buyer's own customer.

## 5. Pre-shipment inspection

Before anything is sealed, goods are checked again: workmanship, packaging, carton counts, and what actually goes into the container. The buyer receives the same report the sourcing team does — raw photos, video, findings — before the balance is due. The decision to ship belongs to the buyer, not the factory. Inspectors are independent third parties who answer to the buyer.

## 6. Freight and logistics

- Ocean freight rates are locked before the buyer commits, so the landed cost stops drifting after the deposit.
- Container loading is watched: count, condition, and seal number documented and photographed before the doors close.
- Shipments are tracked to a major European port; export documents arrive ready for the buyer's customs broker.
- Scheduling is worked backward from the gate-in deadline — production, inland transit, port backlog, blank sailings — not forward from a promise. See https://kalistorik.com/freight.

## The process in four steps

1. Send us your spec — style, materials, quantity, budget, deadline; a photo, drawing, or reference link works.
2. Match the factory, lock the freight — shortlist the workshops that make the product well, and fix the ocean freight rate before commitment.
3. Independent inspection, before you pay — mid-production and pre-shipment checks by third-party inspectors; the balance is released only after the buyer approves.
4. From factory door to your port — container booked, loading watched, seal documented, shipment tracked, export documents prepared.
