# KALIS TORIK — FAQ

The following six questions and answers are identical to the FAQ published at https://kalistorik.com/en/#faq.

## Factories all say they can make my product. How do I know they're actually up to it?

Catalogs don't show real skill. We visit every factory in person before working with them, and match you with workshops that actually build products like yours.

## If nobody checks production, who answers for faulty goods?

Without checks on site, quantity wins over quality. We inspect at the key stages, send you photos and videos each time, and only release payment after you approve.

## My orders are small. Will factories just ignore me?

Big factories often overlook small orders. We connect you with workshops that gladly take trial runs — with the same careful inspection, whatever the size.

## What hidden costs will I pay on top of the factory price?

Low quotes often hide middleman margins. You get one clear all-in quote from us — unit price, inspection, logistics — so nothing surprises you later.

## Can I trust the quality photos factories send me directly?

Factories tend to send flattering photos. Our inspection reports show the real thing: production lines, flaws found, workers, and the sealed container.

## Why buy direct from China instead of using local wholesalers?

Local wholesalers mark up the same Chinese factories heavily. Buying direct keeps more margin in your pocket. Our support is worth paying for — a middleman's markup isn't.
