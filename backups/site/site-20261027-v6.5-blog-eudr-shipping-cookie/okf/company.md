# KALIS TORIK — Company

## What the company is

KALIS TORIK is a B2B sourcing partner for Chinese furniture. The company matches European buyers' specifications to workshops in a network of 100+ vetted factories, keeps people present on the production floor while orders run, and manages inspection and freight through to a major European port. KALIS TORIK has served EU buyers since 2005.

## Who it serves

Small and mid-sized European furniture buyers: furniture retailers, interior studios, e-commerce brands, and contract furniture suppliers. These buyers order in volumes large factories often overlook, run lean teams without regional sourcing offices, and need their goods to arrive sellable and on time.

## How it thinks

The company's starting point: the hard part is not finding a factory — it is finding one that stays honest through the second order, the busy season, and the year nobody is watching. Factories rarely fail loudly; they drift. A queue reshuffles, a finish goes one shade off, a "close enough" goes unmentioned. By unboxing, the window to fix things closed months earlier. The answer KALIS TORIK offers is presence, not promises.

## Facts at a glance

- Founded experience: serving EU buyers since 2005
- Factory network: 100+ factories, each vetted in person, tested with real orders
- Network policy: more factories turned down than taken on; removals are permanent
- Inspection: independent third-party inspectors; buyers receive unfiltered photo and video reports
- Payment: balance released only after the buyer approves the inspection report
- Freight: ocean freight rates locked before commitment; no hidden markups
- Products: Sofas, Beds, Tables, Chairs, Sofa Beds
- Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
