What we do

One partner between your spec and your warehouse

Most European buyers do not need another catalogue. They need someone who can tell whether a factory can actually build the product, whether the sample matches the bulk order, and whether the container will arrive before the selling season is over.

We work on beds, sofas, tables, sofa beds and chairs. The factory list stays short on purpose. Every workshop is visited in person before we place an order, and the ones that slip do not stay on the list.

We are a sourcing and inspection partner. We are not your customs broker, tax adviser or law firm. Those roles stay with the professionals you appoint.

EU entry

The paperwork that decides clearance

Customs does not fail an order because the sofa looks wrong. It fails because a document is missing, a code is wrong, or the importer cannot show where the wood came from. These are the items we track before the container sails.

ItemWhat it coversWho owns it
EORIThe importer number used for EU customs declarations.You, as importer of record; your broker files.
Import VAT and dutyVAT and duty due at entry, based on the customs value and HS code.You; your broker calculates and declares.
EUDRDue diligence for wood and wood-containing products: species, harvest country, supply chain and geolocation where available.You, as operator placing goods on the EU market. We collect factory-side evidence.
GPSRAn EU-based responsible person and traceability information for consumer products.You, with your EU representative; we collect supplier documents.
ISPM 15Heat treatment or fumigation marking for solid-wood packaging.The factory and its packaging supplier; we check the mark before loading.

This table is a working checklist, not legal advice. Rules and dates change; confirm your position with your broker and compliance adviser.

Routing

Ports we plan around

We quote against the route your forwarder can actually hold, not a map pin. The usual gateways for China-origin furniture are below.

NORTH

Antwerp-Bruges · Rotterdam · Hamburg

Deep-water gateways for Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany and onward into France and Scandinavia. Good for full containers and mixed programmes.

WEST

Le Havre · Valencia

Useful for France, Spain and Portugal. Inland haulage and transit time matter as much as the ocean leg.

SOUTH

Genoa · Koper

Options for Italy, Austria and Central Europe. Check congestion and rail connections before booking.

Quality

Where an order is won or lost

Final inspection is too late to fix a Veneer mismatch or a foam substitution. The useful checks happen while the factory can still change the line.

Pre-production sample

We review the first piece, the material and the finish before mass production. You approve what will be copied.

Mid-production

At roughly 30% completion we inspect again. Frame, foam, veneer, stitching and hardware are still fixable at this point.

Pre-shipment

We check quantity, measurements, packing, labels and the loaded container. Photos and video go to you before balance.

Loading

We watch the loading, check the seal and record the container number. The paperwork should match what is inside.

Questions

Europe sourcing FAQ

Do you have a minimum order quantity?
There is no single number. Factories set MOQ by SKU, material and finish. We confirm the real MOQ before quoting, and some workshops will take a trial run.
Can you help with EUDR for wooden furniture?
We collect the factory-side evidence: species, harvest country, the supplier chain, and any harvest geolocation the mill can provide. The due diligence statement remains the EU importer's legal obligation; we help assemble the file, not replace your legal review.
Do I need an EORI and VAT number?
Yes, if you are the importer of record. Your customs broker can confirm the registration you need. We are not a customs broker and do not give tax advice.
Can you inspect the goods before I pay the balance?
Yes. We check the pre-production sample, inspect again at roughly 30% completion, and run a pre-shipment inspection. You receive photos and video before the balance is released.
Which European ports do you ship to?
Common routes arrive at Antwerp-Bruges, Rotterdam, Hamburg, Le Havre, Valencia and Genoa. The forwarder confirms the actual port and routing before we quote.
Can you work with my existing Chinese supplier?
Yes. We can inspect and coordinate an existing factory, or source a new one. The scope and fee depend on what you need us to control.
