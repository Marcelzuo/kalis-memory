# KALIS TORIK — Blog Index

Field notes from the factory floor, written for small furniture buyers who ship across borders. All articles at https://kalistorik.com/blog/.

## You Visit China Twice a Year. Your Orders Run All Twelve Months.

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-local-value
- Published: September 2026
- Topic: On-the-ground value

The months between factory visits are where furniture orders quietly go wrong. The gap after the handshake is not a waiting room: production queues reshuffle, approved samples and mass-production batches diverge, and status reports from a distance describe intentions rather than reality. Distance is not the problem — what distance does to control is. A buyer-side presence on the factory floor closes the gap.

## You Don't Lose an Order at Unboxing. You Lose It Months Earlier.

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-mid-production-qc
- Published: September 2026
- Topic: Quality control

Final inspection only discovers damage that already happened. By the time a carton opens at the buyer's warehouse, every quality decision has been made. The article walks through the checkpoints between deposit and container loading — materials validation before cutting, mid-production inspection while fixes are still cheap, and pre-shipment checks — and what skipping each one costs.

## Your Selling Season Gets Decided While You Sleep

- Published: September 2026
- Topic: Sourcing partner

## The Q4 Shipping Window Is Closing Faster Than Your Booking Confirmation Suggests

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-q4-shipping-window
- Published: September 2026
- Topic: Q4 shipping

A booking confirmation is not a shipping plan. Weather-driven port backlogs, carrier blank sailings, and the holiday shutdown stack on top of each other in Q4, and small importers feel the squeeze first: their single container is the lowest-priority cargo on a rolled vessel. The article lists the confirmations small furniture importers should lock down early, with scheduling worked backward from the gate-in deadline.

## When Your Factory QC Photos Don't Tell the Full Story

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/first-post
- Published: July 2026
- Topic: Quality control

A real case study: factory QC photos of oak-veneer dining tables looked flawless, but they were selective — perfectly lit top-down views that left peeling edge banding, a hairline crack in a leg assembly, and repeated angles out of frame. An independent inspector walking the same production line caught what the photos hid, before the balance payment was released. The lesson: photos do not have to be fake to be dangerous — they only have to be cherry-picked. The article ends with three questions to ask any factory before approving a shipment.

## EUDR for Furniture Importers: What Changes Before Your Next Wooden Order Lands in the EU

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-eudr-wooden-furniture
- Published: October 2026
- Topic: Compliance

The EU Deforestation Regulation moves the paperwork for wooden furniture from the factory to the EU importer of record: the due diligence statement is the obligation of the operator placing goods on the EU market, and no factory export department can take that liability away. The article breaks down the evidence customs will ask for — geolocation of the harvest plots, traceability through every hand-off from forest to mill to assembly factory, proof of legal harvest, and a defensible risk assessment. Because each item lives on factory floors and in local supplier networks, EUDR becomes a sourcing problem before a legal one: request the timber chain in writing and obtain geolocation data before the next order ships.

## Shipping Furniture from China to Europe: What First-Time Importers Get Wrong About the Journey

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-shipping-furniture-china-to-europe
- Published: October 2026
- Topic: Logistics

First-time importers lose money on the journey, not on the product. This buyer-side walkthrough covers the decisions between factory gate and warehouse door. The Incoterm on the quotation is a division of labor and liability: buyers chasing the lowest headline price inherit origin port charges, export documentation and insurance gaps they never priced in. LCL shipments face a consolidation warehouse cut-off earlier than the vessel's; missing it means waiting for the next consolidation, and one late factory delays everyone's cargo in the box. The article lists the documents that decide whether a container moves — commercial invoice and packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and wood packaging declaration — why errors are only fixable before departure.

## Factory Audit Checklist for Furniture Buyers: What to Check Before You Place the First Order

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-factory-audit-checklist
- Published: November 2026
- Topic: Sourcing

A factory audit is not a showroom visit. It verifies who owns the production lines, whether key processes are outsourced, how quality control actually works, and whether the approved sample can be reproduced at order volume. The checklist covers the legal entity and address match, equipment fit for the product category, first-piece confirmation and inspection records, retained samples, and commercial red flags such as a mismatched contracting entity or payment to a personal account. It closes with a pass / conditional pass / fail decision and the controls to write into a trial order.

## Furniture MOQ from China: What Small Buyers Get Wrong About Minimum Order Quantities

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-furniture-moq-china
- Published: November 2026
- Topic: Sourcing

MOQ is not a fixed policy; it is the factory's cost recovery for setup, material batches, finishing cycles, and line time. The number means nothing until the buyer knows whether it is quoted per SKU, per material, or per order — a distinction that routinely turns "50 pieces" into four separate 50-piece minimums. The article covers what actually moves the number (standard colours, mixed SKUs within a series, flexible timing, sample or setup fees, a credible trial-then-repeat plan), the LCL charges that make a low MOQ expensive per unit, the six details to send for a real per-SKU quote, and a trial-review-repeat path instead of a magic number.

## Importing Furniture from China to Belgium: A First-Order Checklist

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-importing-furniture-china-belgium
- Published: November 2026
- Topic: EU Import

The container berthing at Antwerp-Bruges is the last visible step, not the hard part. A first Belgium furniture import is five jobs: identity (who is importer of record, with an EORI and a confirmed VAT position), documents (commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, HS classification, certificate of origin, ISPM 15, EUDR file), compliance (EUDR for wooden furniture and the EU product-safety framework, including an identifiable responsible operator), quality control before shipment, and logistics from the port to the warehouse. The article covers direct versus indirect customs representation, Belgium's 21% standard import VAT, the landed-cost blocks first-time buyers miss, why CE marking is not a blanket furniture requirement, and a ten-item first-order checklist.

## Importing Furniture from China to the US: A First-Order Checklist

- URL: https://kalistorik.com/blog/post-importing-furniture-china-usa
- Published: November 2026
- Topic: US Import

The US is not the EU process with a different flag: no EORI, no import VAT to defer, no CE question — but a customs bond to buy, an ISF filing due before the container leaves China, and a tariff line that can move. The article walks through importer of record and EIN, single-entry versus continuous bond, ISF 10+2 timing, HTSUS classification and Section 301 exposure for Chinese-origin goods, the Lacey Act declaration for wood-containing products, category-specific CPSC rules, and the port-to-warehouse chain (chassis, drayage, detention, dock appointment). It closes with a ten-item first-order checklist and an explicit note that US destination port and service scope are confirmed before quoting.
